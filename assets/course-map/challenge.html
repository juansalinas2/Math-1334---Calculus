How to play

Choose a landing with A–D or tap an answer. Each correct answer earns 1 point plus 2 for each full second left. The clock starts when the question appears. Untimed practice earns 1 point per correct answer. Clear the route to try an optional bonus question. Your streak counts consecutive correct answers and resets on a miss. Class standings show the longest streak for each course, difficulty and question order. In a friend race, everyone gets the same questions and answer positions.
