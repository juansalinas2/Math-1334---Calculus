How to play

Choose a landing with A–D or tap an answer. Each landing earns the base points shown for your difficulty. Timed runs also earn 2 points per full second left. Untimed practice has no time limit. Clear the route to try an optional bonus question. Your streak counts consecutive correct answers and resets on a miss. Open Class best streak to see the shared record and the ten highest timed scores. In a friend race, everyone gets the same questions and answer positions.
